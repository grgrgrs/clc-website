Find out what your neighbors are saying about the proposed federal prison...

 Help us instead of building someplace to lock people up!  We got enough people in jails and prisons already.   Letcher County Resident
There are 500 homeless kids in Letcher County - It is a disgrace on the earth!  Instead of a prison we need housing, flood clean up and road repair.  Letcher County Resident 
I visited prisons as a news reporter.  I saw psychotic people with no treatment.  Letcher County Resident
​Federal funding of this magnitude could help so many people here.  Invest in people and communities - not prisons.  Letcher County Worker
[The prison] will destroy the area and I feel it will not have jobs for local people.  Perry County Resident
We need criminal justice reform- too much incarceration.  West Virginia Resident 
If they build that prison, I am going to move to Tennessee.   Letcher County Resident
