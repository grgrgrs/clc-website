Letcher County Federal Prison Proposal Remains Active After 20 Years
September 17, 2026

A federal prison proposed near Roxana in Letcher County remains active 20 years after it was first announced, with U.S. Rep. Hal Rogers seeking another $100 million beyond the $505 million already appropriated, according to Appalachian Voices. The proposal continues to face local opposition, resistance from presidential administrations of both parties and a land-use obstacle after the Appalachian Rekindling Project acquired a key 63-acre parcel and said it does not intend to sell. For Letcher County residents, the continuing debate raises questions about public spending, land restoration and whether a prison would deliver the promised economic benefits.
