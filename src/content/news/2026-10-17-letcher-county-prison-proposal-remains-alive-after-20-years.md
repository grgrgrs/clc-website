Letcher County prison proposal remains alive after nearly 20 years
September 17, 2026

Appalachian Voices reports that U.S. Rep. Hal Rogers is seeking another $100 million for the proposed federal prison in Letcher County, in addition to the $505 million already appropriated, while local opponents continue to question the project’s progress and promised economic benefits.

The report examines obstacles including the Appalachian Rekindling Project’s purchase of a key 63-acre parcel, which the organization plans to restore using traditional Indigenous ecological practices and by reintroducing bison. It also cites a 2023 Kentucky Center for Economic Policy study that found federal prisons had not improved the economies of three other Southeastern Kentucky counties.
