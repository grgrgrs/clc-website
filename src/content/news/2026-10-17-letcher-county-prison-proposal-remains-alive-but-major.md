September 17, 2026

New reporting from Appalachian Voices finds that the proposed federal prison in Letcher County remains alive after nearly 20 years, as U.S. Rep. Hal Rogers seeks another $100 million on top of the $505 million already appropriated. The proposal still faces local opposition, repeated efforts by presidential administrations to cancel or redirect the funding, and a major land-ownership obstacle after the Appalachian Rekindling Project purchased a key 63-acre parcel and said it will not sell. For Concerned Letcher Countians, the reporting reinforces why lasting public investment should support local people, essential services, land and water—not a costly prison that would bring new risks without demonstrated economic benefit.
