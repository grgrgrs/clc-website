In a new Daily Philosophy article, Raveesh Gupta uses Isaac Asimov’s story “Liar!” to examine AI sycophancy: the tendency of language models to echo our assumptions or validate our views rather than challenge them. His practical response is to create “constructive friction” by asking AI for counterarguments, missing evidence and possible sources of bias—a useful approach for anyone who wants to think more clearly with AI rather than simply receive a reassuring answer.
